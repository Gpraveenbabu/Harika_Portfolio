## 👩‍🔬 About Me

I'm an agricultural economist and digital agriculture specialist. My work centres on coordinating international and EU-funded research and innovation projects, and on evaluating and implementing digital farming solutions.

My academic path runs through India, Belgium, Slovakia and Italy, pairing a strong conceptual grounding in agricultural systems with applied skills in spatial analysis, data analytics and precision agriculture. I work with stakeholders across agricultural value chains to support food system sustainability.

From living labs across Europe to smallholder communities in India, I combine quantitative farm data with participatory, qualitative insight.
