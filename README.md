## 👩‍🔬 About Me

I'm an agricultural economist researching how digital innovation, participatory methods, and sustainable agricultural systems intersect to shape the future of food and farming. My work spans seven countries—India, Slovakia, Belgium, Germany, Italy, the UK, and Slovenia—where I've evaluated 20 European Living Labs, conducted 150 field interviews, and analyzed 250+ farms.

Current focus: *Evolving Agri-Futures: Understanding the dynamics and impacts of digital transition in agricultural systems*
